五維量化評分方法

每隻股票均以100分制進行綜合評分，由五個維度構成，分別針對股息的可持續性、估值吸引力、財務穩健度、增長潛力及技術走勢進行量化評估：

30分
股息質量
評估息率水平、派息比率（Payout Ratio）、過去5年派息紀錄的穩定性，以及自由現金流能否支撐現有股息。若派息比率超過100%或自由現金流為負，會顯著扣分。
25分
估值
透過市盈率（PE）、市帳率（PB）及相對歷史息率，評估股票現價是否具吸引力。估值過高的股票即使息率不錯，上升空間亦受限。
25分
財務健康
審視資產負債表的穩健程度，包括負債比率、流動比率、利息保障倍數及自由現金流。財務脆弱的公司在經濟逆境中容易削減股息。
10分
增長
分析過去3至5年的股息增長趨勢及盈利增長前景。持續增長的股息不僅帶來更高的未來息率，亦反映公司盈利能力的持續改善。
10分
技術走勢
參考RSI相對強弱指數、股價相對52週高低位的位置，以及短中期移動平均線的走勢，評估股票目前的技術面是否有利入市。技術走勢僅作輔助參考，不作主要決策依據。

🔴 高危高危名單說明

高危名單列出評分低於30分的股票，作為投資者的參考警示。高危股通常同時具備以下多項特徵：

The platform covers four major markets: Hong Kong (Hang Seng constituents and major blue chips), United States (S&P 500 high-yield stocks and REITs), United Kingdom (FTSE 100 high-yield stocks and infrastructure trusts), and China A-shares (CSI 300 high-dividend blue chips). We believe that long-term, stable dividend income is a cornerstone of wealth building. Dividend investing provides regular cash flow, and the compounding effect of reinvested dividends significantly boosts long-term returns. However, not all high-yield stocks are worth holding. Some high yields simply reflect the market's concern about a company's outlook or an unsustainable payout. The core mission of this platform is to help investors distinguish between genuine quality dividend stocks and "yield traps."

🔴 High-RiskHigh-Risk List Explained

The High-Risk List highlights stocks scoring below 30 points as a reference warning for investors. High-risk stocks typically exhibit several of the following characteristics simultaneously:

  • Payout ratio far exceeding 100% — dividends are effectively funded by debt or asset sales
  • Negative free cash flow — the company cannot self-fund its dividend from operations
  • Dividend cuts or suspensions in recent years — an unreliable income track record
  • Excessive financial leverage — high debt levels make the company vulnerable in a downturn
  • Severely stretched valuation — the high price has compressed the yield to unattractive levels
  • Persistent earnings decline — the financial basis for maintaining dividends is eroding

The High-Risk List is not a sell recommendation, nor does it imply that a company will definitely face problems. It is an alert tool that flags stocks requiring deeper independent research before any investment decision is made.
